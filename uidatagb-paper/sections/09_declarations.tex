\section*{Code and Data Availability}

All code required to reproduce the analysis, including the Stage~1 pipeline, the Stage~2 leakage correction and follow-up audits, and the LaTeX source of this paper, is publicly available at \url{https://github.com/Xrenes/uidatagb-xai-reliability}. The repository is organised into three top-level directories:

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{fedgb/} --- the main Stage~1 analysis pipeline: dataset handling, the perceptual-hash de-duplication split correction (Section~\ref{sec:methods}.1), all five XAI methods, and the full quantitative reliability battery. Principal entry points include \texttt{dedupe\_split\_uidatagb.py} (emits \texttt{outputs/phase0/leakage\_check.json} and \texttt{outputs/phase0/dedupe\_split\_report.json}), \texttt{gradcam.py} (Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, Saliency, Eigen-CAM, and Score-CAM classes), \texttt{compute\_consistency.py} (cross-replicate cosine consistency $O_r^c$; emits \texttt{outputs/phase0/consistency.json}), \texttt{analyze\_failure\_cases\_uidatagb.py} (Section~\ref{sec:results}.2's confusion matrix and failure-case figures), \texttt{compute\_faithfulness.py}, \texttt{compute\_stability.py}, \texttt{sanity\_checks.py}, \texttt{compute\_calibration.py}, and \texttt{shortcut\_audit.py} (the reliability battery of Section~\ref{sec:results}.4), and \texttt{generate\_uidatagb\_figures.py}/\texttt{generate\_xai\_galleries.py} (figure regeneration). \texttt{verify\_batch\_acquisition\_evidence.py} and \texttt{phash\_threshold\_sensitivity.py} produce the acquisition-timestamp and threshold-sensitivity evidence of Section~\ref{sec:followup-audits}.
\item \texttt{kaggle\_retrain/} --- Kaggle-GPU scripts for the Stage~2 cluster-aware retrain (\texttt{uidatagb\_full\_retrain.py}: the corrected 70/20/10 split, same-data replicate seeds, and the consistency null controls of Section~\ref{sec:stage2}) and the follow-up shortcut and noise audits of Section~\ref{sec:followup-audits} (\texttt{uidatagb\_stage2\_remaining\_reviews.py}: the leakage-controlled batch-ID diagnostic, the linear probe on the disease classifier's own features, and the noise-robustness sweep).
\item \texttt{uidatagb-paper/} --- the IEEEtran LaTeX source for this manuscript.
\end{itemize}

Small JSON and log result files that the paper's reported numbers are computed from are included under \texttt{fedgb/outputs/phase0/} and \texttt{fedgb/outputs/frozen\_results/}; \texttt{fedgb/outputs/frozen\_results/results\_master.csv} traces every reported number to its source file, and \texttt{fedgb/paper/claim\_evidence\_matrix.md} maps every quantitative claim to its evidence and stated limitation boundary. Dataset images and trained model checkpoints are not redistributed in the repository, both because of their size and because dataset redistribution terms have not yet been confirmed with the original providers (Limitation~4c). The dataset itself is the publicly released UIdataGB collection \cite{ref17}. Source code is licensed MIT.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
This study uses the publicly released, fully anonymized UIdataGB ultrasound dataset \cite{ref17}, collected and released for public research use by the original data providers. No new patient data were collected, and no identifiable patient information was accessed at any stage of this work. Since the public release contains no patient- or study-level identifiers, full patient-level split independence could not be independently verified in this analysis (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Separate institutional ethics board approval was not required for this secondary, fully-anonymized analysis.
